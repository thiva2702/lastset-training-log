# LastSet v2 expansion backlog

These ideas are intentionally outside the v1 feature freeze. They can be considered for LastSet 2.0 or a future paid tier after the core training logger is stable.

## Home and progression experience

- Replace the current Today centric entry experience with a dedicated Home screen when ready.
- Home can show today's workout, recent progress, streaks, personal records and weekly consistency.
- Add an animated LastSet character that evolves with real training progression and consistency.
- Character growth should remain believable and reward long term effort rather than exaggerating short term changes.
- Unlock poses, outfits, environments, badges or cosmetic rewards through training milestones.

## Progress photos

- Allow users to save mirror selfies as private progress photos.
- Support comparable dates, consistent crop and optional pose guidance.
- Add before and after comparison or a swipe slider.
- Consider on device assistance for framing and alignment.
- Future paid option: physique change summaries or trend analysis, only with clear privacy controls.

## Advanced analytics

- Weekly and monthly training insights.
- Muscle group coverage and recovery views.
- Volume, intensity and estimated strength trends where mathematically appropriate.
- Personal record timeline.
- Planned versus completed workout comparisons.
- Longer term adherence and consistency scoring.

## AI and coaching

- More capable workout interpretation and correction.
- Adaptive workout recommendations based on history, progression and recovery.
- Advanced coaching explanations rather than only next set recommendations.
- General machine recognition when reliable visual recognition is available.

## Accounts, sync and devices

- Optional account based cloud sync.
- Multi device restore and migration.
- Apple Health and wearable integrations where useful.
- Optional workout data import from supported fitness ecosystems.

## Equipment expansion

- User configurable bar weights for Olympic bar, EZ Bar, trap bar and specialty bars.
- Plate calculator and plates only versus total load entry preference.
- Landmine, suspension trainer, trap bar and additional specialty equipment.
- Custom user exercises and custom equipment definitions.

## Social and engagement

- Optional challenges, achievements and streak goals.
- Private friend comparisons or shared challenges only if they add value without turning LastSet into a social feed.

## Possible paid tier candidates

Core workout logging, Calendar, Saved Workouts, basic Progress and reliable local data should remain useful without payment.

Potential paid features include advanced analytics, cloud sync, advanced AI coaching, richer photo progression tools, avatar customisation and evolution systems, longer term automated insights, and premium integrations.

## Product rule

A v2 feature should earn its place by improving motivation, insight or convenience. It should not weaken the speed, accuracy or simplicity of the v1 training logger.
